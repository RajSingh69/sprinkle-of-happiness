Understanding Delusions and Schizophrenia Circle

This circle is planned and not yet running. It will only start once trained facilitators and safeguarding arrangements are confirmed.

Compassionate stigma-reducing educational / peer-support space

A space around psychosis, delusions and schizophrenia focused on listening, dignity, respectful language and safety.

Circle of care

Conversation, listening and choice held with dignity.

What we'll explore

MythsCommunicationGroundingSupport networksWhen professional help may be needed

What you might do

EducationPeer reflectionRespectful discussionGrounding ideasSignposting

Who it may be suitable for

People seeking compassionate, stigma-reducing understanding.

What to expect

A careful, bounded circle focused on support rather than argument.

Choice and participation

You can speak, listen, pass or step away.

Wellbeing boundary

This circle does not debate specific beliefs, validate specific delusional beliefs, diagnose or provide treatment.
