I Am a Survivor: Storytelling Workshop

Survivor-led storytelling workshop

Explore survival, recovery, strength, voice and agency through creative forms.

Creative reflection

A warm space for writing, imagination and personal meaning.

What we'll explore

SurvivalRecoveryStrengthVoiceAgency

What you might do

PoetryMemoirSpoken wordFictional distanceMetaphorPerformance

Who it may be suitable for

People who want a choice-led storytelling space that honours agency and control.

What to expect

Prompts that support voice without forcing disclosure or trauma retelling.

Choice and participation

You may write privately, use a fictional character, share only part of something or simply listen.

Wellbeing boundary

The purpose is not to force people to relive trauma. Participants choose how they participate.
