How to join

Booking isn't open yet. Venues and booking for December events will be announced here and on Instagram. Until then, open any event in the calendar and use Email to register interest: the email names the event and date for you. An email isn't a booked place, but we'll let you know as soon as booking opens.

We only ask for your name and how to reach you. You never need to share health details or personal history to take part.

You don't need a paid membership for any of these events. A free account lets you choose email updates.
