New to yoga?

You do not need to be flexible, spiritual, confident or experienced to begin.

Sprinkle of Happiness yoga is being shaped as analogue wellness: presence over performance, breath over pressure, and community over comparison.

You can pause, adapt, ask questions, sit out, or simply observe while you find your way in.

A letter from Kaan

Yoga, for me, is not about performing wellness.

It is about returning to the body with gentleness, listening more deeply, learning with humility and creating space for breath, prayer, reflection, movement and community.

Even in difficult times, practice can become one small way of staying connected to life.
