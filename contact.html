Accessibility

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website and take part in our community. Here is what we've done so far, what we know still needs work, and how to tell us about any difficulty.

On this website

  • Pages are built to work on phones, tablets and computers, from small 360px screens upwards.
  • You can move around with a keyboard. Menus open and close with Enter and Escape, and the item you're on is clearly outlined.
  • Images have text descriptions for screen readers, and decorative images are hidden from them.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off or kept to a minimum.
  • Crisis-support details are available from every page.

What we know still needs work

  • There is no "skip to main content" link yet.
  • Some pages use soft colours and decorative backgrounds, and we haven't yet checked the colour contrast of every page.
  • Booking and payment pages provided by other services, such as Calendly and Stripe, are outside our control.

We haven't had this website formally audited against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), so we don't claim it meets them.

At our sessions and events

Accessibility details for each session, such as venue access, will be shared as they are confirmed. If you have access needs, or would like information in another format, please tell us before you come and we'll do our best to help.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website doesn't work for you, please email hello@sprinkleofhappiness.co.uk or use the contact form above and choose Accessibility. Tell us which page and what happened, and we'll reply when we can.
