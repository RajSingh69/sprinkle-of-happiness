A conversation with Kaan

A relaxed, informal chat. Not counselling, therapy or crisis support.

You do not need to arrive with the right words.

Coffee with Kaan is an informal conversation about Sprinkle of Happiness, community, creativity and ways to get involved. It is not counselling, therapy or crisis support.

If you are struggling with suicidal thoughts, please contact the services below or visit our Need Support page.

Ask about a conversation

This is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

To ask about a coffee, talk or walk with Kaan, send a short email. Kaan may not be able to reply quickly, and sending an email does not book or confirm a conversation.

If you may act on suicidal thoughts or cannot keep yourself safe, please do not wait for a reply. Call 999, go to A&E, or call Samaritans on 116 123.
