Ask about a conversation

This is not an emergency or crisis-response service.

To ask about a coffee, talk or walk with Kaan, send a short email. Kaan may not be able to reply quickly, and sending an email does not book or confirm a conversation.

If you may act on suicidal thoughts or cannot keep yourself safe, please do not wait for a reply. Call 999, go to A&E, or call Samaritans on 116 123.

Email to ask about Coffee with Kaan

hello@sprinkleofhappines.org.uk

Please keep it brief: your name, how you would like to be contacted, and whether you would prefer a talk or a walk.
