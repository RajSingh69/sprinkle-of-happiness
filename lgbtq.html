LGBTQ+ at Sprinkle

A LGBTQ+ Safe Space

At Sprinkle of Happiness, you do not have to explain yourself before you are welcome.

This is a space for LGBTQ+ people who are questioning, proud, spiritual, creative, healing, joyful, navigating something difficult, or simply looking for community.

We hold space for the fullness of queer life: the dreams, the secrets, the faith, the family stories, the recovery, and the futures still waiting to be imagined.

Why we are an LGBTQ+ safe space

Inclusion is part of how we create, not an afterthought.

LGBTQ+ perspectives are intended to shape how Sprinkle of Happiness designs and facilitates its workshops, circles and community work. Groups are run with agreements around respect, consent, privacy and choice.

Sharing is always optional, and we ask everyone to respect each other's privacy. Your facilitator will explain confidentiality, including when we may need to act to keep someone safe, at the start of each session.

  • People who are questioning
  • People who are closeted, or out to some and not others
  • People who are newly out
  • People navigating faith, culture and family alongside their identity
  • People holding many identities at once

You are not too much. You are not too complicated. You belong here.

LGBTQ+ workshops & circles

Six ways in, at your own pace.

Sharing is always optional. You never have to disclose your identity or your history to take part.

Workshop

Queer Dreams & Futures

What do you dream of when you allow yourself to imagine freely?

A creative workshop using journalling, reflection and gentle creative exercises to explore the dreams and futures that can open up beyond difficult past experiences.

  • What do I truly desire?
  • What future feels possible?
  • What have I been taught to want?
  • What would life look like if I believed I deserved joy?

Come as you are. Leave with more room to dream.

12-week workshop

Queer Artist's Way

A 12-week creative journey through a queer lens. Inspired by Julia Cameron's The Artist's Way and adapted by Kaan to explore LGBTQ+ identity, creativity, self-expression and community.

  • Creative blocks
  • Shame and expectations
  • Journalling and reflection
  • Reconnecting with creative identity
  • Play and self-belief
  • Supportive queer creative community

This is Kaan's independent adaptation. It is not an official programme and is not endorsed by Julia Cameron.

Circle

Queer Faithful Circle

A reflective circle for LGBTQ+ people who practise, question, love or struggle with faith. Welcoming people of every faith, and anyone reconsidering what they believe.

  • Being queer and religious
  • Family, tradition and belonging
  • Faith after rejection or shame
  • Affirming spiritual practices
  • Relationships with the divine
  • Holding belief and doubt together

This is not about convincing anyone to believe or leave. It is about making room for your whole self.

Queer Panic Attack Circle

Planned — coming soon

What does a queer panic attack feel like to you?

A gentle peer space for conversation, grounding, creative reflection and community around anxiety and overwhelm.

  • Anxiety and overwhelm
  • Masking and hypervigilance
  • Rejection and discrimination
  • Family tension
  • Compassion and grounding

This circle is peer support and creative reflection. It is not therapy, medical treatment or crisis support, and it cannot prevent or treat panic attacks. If you need help now, visit Need Support?
