Privacy Notice

For Sprinkle of Happiness member accounts · Version 1 · 17 October 2026 · This notice is under review and may be updated.

Who we are

Sprinkle of Happiness Holistic Creative Arts Factory CIC ("we", "us") is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at hello@sprinkleofhappiness.co.uk.

Our company registration number and registered office address are being confirmed and will be added here.

What we collect when you create an account

  • Your name: the name you choose to display.
  • Your email address.
  • Your password. It is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. We never see it.
  • Account records: when your account was created and updated, whether your email is verified, and your membership tier (Free).
  • Security data that our authentication provider processes to keep accounts safe, such as sign-in times.

We do not ask for health information, details of personal crises, your date of birth or payment details when you create an account. Please don't include sensitive information in your display name.

Why we use it

  • To provide your account and member dashboard (to perform our agreement with you).
  • To send essential account emails, such as email verification and password reset (to perform our agreement with you).
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent misuse (our legitimate interests).
  • If you choose to, to send you membership updates and community news by email (your consent).

Email updates (optional)

Email updates are optional and separate from your account. The box is unticked unless you tick it. When you choose, we record your choice, the wording you saw, its version and the time. You can change your mind at any time in My Account, or with the unsubscribe link in any update email. Withdrawing consent does not affect your account. If we send a welcome email when your account is created, it is part of setting up your membership, not marketing.

Donations

If you donate online, your payment is handled by our payment provider, Stripe, which processes your card and billing details under its own privacy notice. We only keep the amount, whether it is one-off or monthly, and the date. We never see or store your card details.

Who processes it for us

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores and secures member accounts on our behalf. Google may process data outside the UK under its contractual safeguards for international transfers.
  • Stripe processes paid membership payments and, once they open, online donations.
  • Calendly handles appointments booked with Kaan, under its own privacy notice. Booking times are only loaded on our Book a Session page if you choose to show them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself. No account data is stored there.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account data while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account in My Account, your profile, email preferences and login are deleted. We keep a minimal deletion record (your account ID and the time of deletion, with no name or email) so the deleted account cannot be recreated.
  • If an account is never verified, it is kept until you ask us to delete it. We will review how long unverified accounts are kept.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or for a copy of it. You can delete your account yourself at any time in My Account, or email us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Safety

Sprinkle of Happiness is not a crisis or emergency service. If you are in danger, call 999. You can call Samaritans any time on 116 123.
