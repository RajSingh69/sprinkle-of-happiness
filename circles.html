Community agreements

Make space for yourself and others.

Sprinkle of Happiness activities offer creative, community and peer connection. They are not a replacement for therapy, medical treatment or emergency mental-health support.

Share only what feels safe and appropriate.Confidentiality is respected, with limits where there is serious risk of harm.Listen without judgement or interruption.No unsolicited medical or therapeutic advice.Speak from your own experience.Respect identities, beliefs, access needs and communication styles.You can take a break, pass or leave.Make space for everyone.
