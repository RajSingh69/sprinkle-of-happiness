Our story

Creativity as a doorway.

Sprinkle of Happiness was created in response to the spaces we could not find: spaces that were affordable, creative, culturally aware, non-judgemental and open to everyone.

We use creativity as a doorway into reflection, expression and connection. Our work may include poetry, journalling, storytelling, visual arts, movement, film, music and community conversation.

Our mission

Creative wellbeing. Community first.

Our mission is to create accessible, human spaces where people can explore mental wellbeing without judgement, pressure or expectation.

Who we support

Come as you are.

Sprinkle of Happiness is designed around free or accessible, creative and community-led wellbeing support.

The space is intended to be culturally aware, welcoming and non-judgemental, allowing people to explore creativity, connection and self-expression in their own way.
