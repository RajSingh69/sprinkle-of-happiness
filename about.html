Our vision

To create a world where no one has to heal alone.

Where creativity, spirituality and community help people move from survival towards hope, connection and joy.

Treated with dignityEncouraged to express themselvesSupported to recognise their own strength

Creative wellbeing should be accessible to everyone, especially people and communities who have experienced trauma, isolation, discrimination or mental-health challenges.

Who we welcome

There is a place for you here.

Sprinkle of Happiness welcomes trauma survivors, sensitive souls, creatives, and people experiencing stress, loneliness, life changes or emotional overwhelm.

Black communitiesAsian communitiesSouth Asian communitiesSWANA communitiesLGBTQ+ communitiesNeurodivergent communities
The whole person

At Sprinkle of Happiness, we hold space for the whole person.

The difficult partsThe hopeful partsThe creative partsEverything in between

Whether you come to write, move, rest, share your story, meet new people or simply spend time somewhere you feel welcome, there is a place for you here.

Healing can be creative. Community can be nourishing.

And even in difficult times, we can find small ways to stay in Chardi Kala: moving towards hope, courage and aliveness.
