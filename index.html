Meet Kaan

Built from lived experience.

"I created Sprinkle of Happiness from a deeply personal place...

Founder Kaan Singh is a writer, performer, photographer and creative facilitator. The work grew from his lived experiences of trauma, mental health challenges, homelessness, survival, recovery, spirituality and creative expression.
