Partnerships

Up for a Good Cause? Partner With Us

Sprinkle of Happiness Holistic Creative Arts Factory CIC is a community-led organisation bringing together creativity, movement, reflection and connection, so that more people can find a place to belong.

Email us about partneringWays to get involved
Why partner with us

Collaboration helps creative wellbeing reach more people.

Working together, partners can help us offer more and reach further.

  • Accessible creative wellbeing activities
  • Community workshops and circles
  • Opportunities for people experiencing isolation or emotional challenges
  • Creative expression and belonging
  • Community-led projects across London and Southall
What we offer partners

Three ways a partnership can grow.

Visibility and reach

Potential recognition on event materials, our website, newsletters and social media, agreed with you in advance.

Meaningful social impact

Opportunities to sponsor free places, materials, workshops, community activities and outreach.

Creative collaboration

Joint workshops, poetry, storytelling, photography, yoga, walks, exhibitions and community events.

Yoga studios and teacher training

Partnerships With Yoga Studios

We would love to work with yoga studios and Yoga Teacher Training (YTT) providers. Sprinkle of Happiness could offer a warm, welcoming setting where trainee teachers gain supportive first teaching experiences.

Any arrangement would be agreed together. No placements, accreditation or supervision are offered or guaranteed yet.

Discuss a Yoga Studio Partnership
  • A welcoming setting for new yoga teachers
  • Confidence-building through appropriately supported teaching
  • Collaboration with training providers and studios
  • Respect for yoga's traditional roots and teacher–student relationships
  • An open conversation about practical arrangements
