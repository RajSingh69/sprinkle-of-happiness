Rebel Recovery & Freedom

Recovery Without Shame. Freedom Without Conditions.

Rebel Recovery & Freedom is a recovery-oriented peer-support and creative wellbeing approach developed by Kaan Singh. It welcomes people exploring addictive or compulsive patterns, whether around substances, gambling, spending, relationships, work or other behaviours. Every person's experience is different, and these patterns do not all share the same causes.

  • Recovery without shame
  • Accountability without humiliation
  • Compassionate exploration of patterns
  • Community, reflection and personal agency
  • Bring your identity, culture, faith and queerness with you

This is a peer-support and reflective approach, not medical or psychological treatment. Read our safety information.

Our approach

Three ways of looking gently at patterns.

Reflection

Understanding patterns

Kaan's approach invites you to look at the relationship between habits, feelings, coping strategies and the circumstances of your life, with curiosity rather than judgement.

What is this behaviour protecting me from — and what is it asking me to heal?

This is a journalling prompt to explore, not an explanation of addiction. Not every pattern comes from pain, and you decide what fits.

Sikh-inspired wisdom

The Five Thieves

The programme draws inspiration from the Five Thieves in Sikhi, a spiritual framework for reflecting on what can pull us away from ourselves.

  • Kaam — lust or uncontrolled desire
  • Krodh — anger
  • Lobh — greed
  • Moh — attachment
  • Ahankar — ego

These are offered as a lens for reflection, not as medical diagnoses or causes of addiction. People of all faiths and none are welcome.

Writing

Shadow journalling

A reflective writing practice for noticing patterns, emotions, beliefs and experiences you might usually look away from.

  • Shame and self-judgement
  • Grief and loneliness
  • Anger and fear
  • Attachment and belonging
  • Habits and triggers
  • Personal boundaries
  • Hope and change

You are never required to write about or disclose traumatic experiences, or to complete any exercise that feels too intense.

Planned approach

Accountability partners, not sponsors

Kaan's model pairs people with an accountability partner who may help with:

  • Regular check-ins
  • Realistic intentions
  • Recognising patterns
  • Encouragement
  • Celebrating progress
  • Returning to support after setbacks

The intention is that nobody is shamed or turned away after a relapse. Partner matching, availability and boundaries are still being developed; there is no matching service yet.

Accountability without shame

You can take responsibility and still be treated with dignity.

Accountability

Recognising your behaviour, taking responsibility, repairing harm where appropriate, and choosing a constructive next step.

Shame

Feeling fundamentally unworthy, or believing you are defined forever by what you have done.

We hold onto accountability and let go of shame. That does not guarantee recovery or prevent relapse, but it can make it easier to keep coming back.

What a circle looks like

Six gentle steps.

  1. Welcome and grounding

    Arriving and settling in.

  2. Optional check-in

    Share only what feels comfortable, or nothing at all.

  3. Reflection and journalling

    Exploring a topic or prompt together.

  4. Circle sharing

    Voluntary, always with the option to pass.

  5. Practical next steps

    Intentions for the week, and useful resources.

  6. Closing

    Encouragement and connection before you go.

Sharing is always optional. We ask everyone to respect each other's privacy and to keep phones away where possible, unless you need yours for access, safety or emergencies. Your facilitator will explain confidentiality, including when we may need to act to keep someone safe, before each circle begins.

Is this right for me?

It may suit you if you…

Rebel Recovery & Freedom will not be right for everyone or for every situation. If you need medical detoxification, withdrawal management or specialist addiction treatment, please seek qualified healthcare support first. A circle can sit alongside that support, never in place of it.

  • Want recovery-oriented support without judgement
  • Have not felt comfortable in traditional recovery spaces
  • Are drawn to creativity, reflection and community
  • Value spirituality or Sikh-inspired wisdom
  • Want an LGBTQ+-affirming space
  • Are exploring your relationship with substances or behaviours
Safety and support

Please read this first.

Rebel Recovery & Freedom is a peer-support and creative wellbeing programme. It is not a substitute for medical detoxification, addiction treatment, therapy or emergency care.

Stopping suddenly can be dangerous. Suddenly stopping alcohol or certain drugs can cause serious withdrawal that may need medical supervision. Please speak to your GP, NHS 111 or a specialist service before stopping, and do not try to manage serious withdrawal on your own.

A peer-support circle cannot safely manage a crisis. If you are in crisis, please get urgent help rather than waiting for a circle.

  • 999Immediate danger or a medical emergency
  • NHS 111Urgent advice. In England, choose the mental-health option
  • Samaritans 116 123Emotional support, any time, free
