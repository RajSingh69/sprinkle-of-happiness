The Year of Becoming

Four seasons. One becoming.

A year-long journey that begins with our launch on 1 December 2026. Each season has its own focus, each month its own invitation, and each week its own theme.

December 2026 to February 2027

Winter Let go

Reflection, examining old patterns and making space for change.

  1. DecemberFace it
  2. JanuaryFeel it
  3. FebruaryFree it

Winter, week by week

Each week has a theme and a question to sit with. There are no right answers, and you never have to share yours.

December · Face it

  1. Week 1Arrive1–6 DecemberWhat did it take for me to get here today?
  2. Week 2The Inventory7–13 DecemberWhat am I carrying that was never mine?
  3. Week 3Meet Your Shadow14–20 DecemberWhat do I judge in others that I hide in myself?
  4. Week 4The Longest Night21–27 DecemberWhat does my darkness need from me tonight?
  5. Week 5Quantum Leap28 December to 3 JanuaryWho am I when I stop being who I had to be?
