How membership will work

Join free, and add support if you want it.

  1. Join for free

    Create your free account and verify your email.

  2. Choose a paid tier, if you like

    Spark, Glow or Radiance are optional.

  3. Pay securely

    Paid tiers will be paid through Stripe.

  4. Receive onboarding

    You will be sent instructions for getting started.

  5. Manage or cancel

    Change or cancel a paid tier at any time.

Free registration is open now. Spark, Glow and Radiance are paid securely through Stripe, and you can cancel any time in My Account. To ask about membership, email hello@sprinkleofhappiness.co.uk.

Questions

Membership FAQs

Is membership free?

Yes. Free membership is the main way to join the Sprinkle of Happiness community. Spark, Glow and Radiance are optional paid memberships for people who want extra support.

Are yoga and workshops really free?

Yes. Yoga and workshops are free and open to everyone. Membership is an optional extra for people who want to go deeper.

How is membership priced?

Free membership costs nothing. Optional paid memberships are monthly subscriptions: Spark £27, Glow £97 and Radiance £497 per month.

How will I pay?

Free membership needs no payment. For paid tiers, payments will be taken securely through Stripe once registration opens. No payments are being taken yet.

Can I cancel?

You will be able to manage or cancel your subscription. Full cancellation and refund terms will be published before registration opens.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

We intend to make it possible to move between tiers. How and when changes take effect will be confirmed before launch.

Is membership online?

Many member benefits, such as the community space, calls and audio library, are expected to be online. Details of each benefit's format will be shared when registration opens.

What if I miss a session?

Our approach to missed sessions, including whether any sessions are recorded or can be rescheduled, will be confirmed before launch.

Why is Radiance limited?

Radiance includes weekly 1:1 time with Kaan, so only a limited number of places can be offered at any one time.

Are there reduced-rate places or scholarships?

We would love to make membership more accessible. Whether reduced-rate places or scholarships will be offered has not yet been decided. Free yoga and workshops remain open to all.

How will I get access after joining?

Free members can use My Account as soon as their email is verified. Access details for paid memberships will be shared when they open.

Become a member. It's free.

Free registration is open now. Questions about membership? Email hello@sprinkleofhappiness.co.uk. In the meantime, our yoga and workshops are free for everyone.
