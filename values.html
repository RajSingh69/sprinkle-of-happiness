Our Values

Turning spiritual reflection into everyday action.

Listening with compassion. Creating with honesty. Serving with humility. Choosing hope together.

Sikh inspiration

Inspired by Sikh values. Welcoming people of all faiths and no faith.

Sprinkle of Happiness is inspired by equality, compassion, service, truthful living and Chardi Kala: maintaining courage, hope and a positive spirit through life's challenges.

This work is not about perfection, or pretending difficult emotions do not exist. It is about recognising inner struggles while nurturing greater awareness, kindness and courage.

Reflection

The Five Thieves

In Sikhi, these describe inner tendencies that can cloud judgement and draw someone away from their spiritual and ethical path. They are not labels for judging people. They are invitations to notice what may be influencing our thoughts, feelings and actions.

Kaam

Uncontrolled desire or lust.

Responding with awareness, respect and self-control.

Krodh

Anger or rage.

Transforming anger into honest, compassionate action.

Lobh

Greed or endless wanting.

Generosity, gratitude and enoughness.

Moh

Attachment or clinging.

Loving deeply while accepting change, loss and uncertainty.

Ahankaar

Ego, pride or inflated sense of self.

Humility, openness and connection.

How we live them

Spiritual reflection becomes everyday care.

Truth

Real stories including complexity, pain and hope.

Compassion

Meeting people where they are rather than expecting them to perform wellness.

Contentment

A small creative moment, cup of tea or genuine conversation can matter.

Humility

Learning from communities and not claiming to have all the answers.

Love

Creating spaces where people feel welcomed, respected and valued.

Seva

Offering time, care, creativity and practical support.

Equality

Every person has equal worth.

Chardi Kala

Nurturing courage, hope and belief in brighter days.
